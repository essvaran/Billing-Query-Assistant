# Shipping SLA Matrix

## Delivery commitments by region

| Region | Name | Standard delivery | Express delivery | Order cutoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | United States | 3 business days | 1 business days | 17:00 ET |
| CA | Canada | 5 business days | 2 business days | 15:00 ET |
| UK | United Kingdom | 4 business days | 2 business days | 16:00 GMT |
| EU | European Union | 6 business days | 3 business days | 15:00 CET |
| AU | Australia | 8 business days | 4 business days | 14:00 AEST |
| APAC | Asia Pacific | 10 business days | 5 business days | 14:00 SGT |

## Notes

Business days exclude weekends and public holidays in the destination region. Orders placed
after the cutoff ship the next business day. These commitments apply to every account plan.
