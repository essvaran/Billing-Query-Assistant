# Tax and VAT on invoices (web)

## Overview

How tax is calculated and shown on the website. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The tax line is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 5 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the tax vat option on the website.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the tax line request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 9 |
| Tax line valid for | 5 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-1131, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
9 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-1131.
