# Single sign-on setup (team)

## Overview

Connect your identity provider for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The SSO connection is issued within 60 minutes and stays valid for 9 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the sso option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the SSO connection request.
4. Wait up to 60 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 6 |
| Sso connection valid for | 9 days |
| Typical completion time | 60 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-1410, wait 60 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
6 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-1410.
