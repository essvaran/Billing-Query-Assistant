# Currency and exchange rates (troubleshooting)

## Overview

Which currency you are charged in when something goes wrong. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The exchange rate is issued within 15 minutes and stays valid for 9 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the currency option when something goes wrong.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the exchange rate request.
4. Wait up to 15 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 6 |
| Exchange rate valid for | 9 days |
| Typical completion time | 15 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-6741, wait 15 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
6 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-6741.
