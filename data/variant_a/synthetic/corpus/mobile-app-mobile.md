# Use the mobile app (mobile)

## Overview

Install and sign in on a phone in the mobile app. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The app sign-in is issued within 10 minutes and stays valid for 7 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the mobile app option in the mobile app.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the app sign-in request.
4. Wait up to 10 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 8 |
| App sign-in valid for | 7 days |
| Typical completion time | 10 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-3002, wait 10 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
8 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-3002.
