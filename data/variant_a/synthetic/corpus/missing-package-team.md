# Package marked delivered but missing (team)

## Overview

Steps when tracking says delivered for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The investigation is issued within 5 minutes and stays valid for 8 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the missing package option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the investigation request.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 6 |
| Investigation valid for | 8 days |
| Typical completion time | 5 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-5889, wait 5 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
6 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-5889.
