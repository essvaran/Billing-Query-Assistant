# Change the delivery address (team)

## Overview

Edit the address before the parcel ships for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The address change is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 9 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the change address option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the address change request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 10 |
| Address change valid for | 9 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-6414, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
10 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-6414.
