# Use the mobile app (team)

## Overview

Install and sign in on a phone for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The app sign-in is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 5 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the mobile app option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the app sign-in request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 8 |
| App sign-in valid for | 5 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-3932, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
8 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-3932.
