# Pause your subscription (mobile)

## Overview

Pause billing without losing your data in the mobile app. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The pause window is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 9 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the plan pause option in the mobile app.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the pause window request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 12 |
| Pause window valid for | 9 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-8211, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
12 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-8211.
