# Change the email on your account (web)

## Overview

Update the sign-in and receipt email address on the website. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The confirmation message is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 3 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the change email option on the website.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the confirmation message request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 10 |
| Confirmation message valid for | 3 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-6933, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
10 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-6933.
