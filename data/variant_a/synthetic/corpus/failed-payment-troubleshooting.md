# Fix a failed payment (troubleshooting)

## Overview

Understand why a charge failed and retry it when something goes wrong. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The retry attempt is issued within 10 minutes and stays valid for 6 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the failed payment option when something goes wrong.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the retry attempt request.
4. Wait up to 10 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 6 |
| Retry attempt valid for | 6 days |
| Typical completion time | 10 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-7401, wait 10 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
6 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-7401.
