# Export your data (web)

## Overview

Download a copy of your account data on the website. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The export archive is issued within 10 minutes and stays valid for 6 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the data export option on the website.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the export archive request.
4. Wait up to 10 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 9 |
| Export archive valid for | 6 days |
| Typical completion time | 10 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-8615, wait 10 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
9 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-8615.
