# Enterprise invoicing and purchase orders (team)

## Overview

Pay by purchase order and net terms for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The purchase order is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 3 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the enterprise invoicing option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the purchase order request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 9 |
| Purchase order valid for | 3 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-6076, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
9 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-6076.
