# How long delivery takes (team)

## Overview

Understand delivery estimates for your order for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The delivery estimate is issued within 10 minutes and stays valid for 3 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the delivery times option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the delivery estimate request.
4. Wait up to 10 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 9 |
| Delivery estimate valid for | 3 days |
| Typical completion time | 10 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-7240, wait 10 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
9 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-7240.
