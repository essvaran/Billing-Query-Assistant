# Add or remove team seats (mobile)

## Overview

Manage seats on a team plan in the mobile app. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The seat change is issued within 15 minutes and stays valid for 4 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the team seats option in the mobile app.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the seat change request.
4. Wait up to 15 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 10 |
| Seat change valid for | 4 days |
| Typical completion time | 15 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-7188, wait 15 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
10 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-7188.
