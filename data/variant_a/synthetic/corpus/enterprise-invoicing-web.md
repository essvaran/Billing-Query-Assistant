# Enterprise invoicing and purchase orders (web)

## Overview

Pay by purchase order and net terms on the website. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The purchase order is issued within 5 minutes and stays valid for 2 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the enterprise invoicing option on the website.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the purchase order request.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 11 |
| Purchase order valid for | 2 days |
| Typical completion time | 5 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-1359, wait 5 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
11 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-1359.
