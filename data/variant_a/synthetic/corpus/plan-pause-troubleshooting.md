# Pause your subscription (troubleshooting)

## Overview

Pause billing without losing your data when something goes wrong. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The pause window is issued within 15 minutes and stays valid for 9 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the plan pause option when something goes wrong.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the pause window request.
4. Wait up to 15 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 8 |
| Pause window valid for | 9 days |
| Typical completion time | 15 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-9793, wait 15 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
8 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-9793.
