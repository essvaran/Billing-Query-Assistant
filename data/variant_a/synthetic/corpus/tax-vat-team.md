# Tax and VAT on invoices (team)

## Overview

How tax is calculated and shown for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The tax line is issued within 10 minutes and stays valid for 8 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the tax vat option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the tax line request.
4. Wait up to 10 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 11 |
| Tax line valid for | 8 days |
| Typical completion time | 10 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-3143, wait 10 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
11 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-3143.
