# Earn and spend loyalty points (web)

## Overview

How points accrue and expire on the website. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The points balance is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 6 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the loyalty points option on the website.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the points balance request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 7 |
| Points balance valid for | 6 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-9535, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
7 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-9535.
