# Use the mobile app (troubleshooting)

## Overview

Install and sign in on a phone when something goes wrong. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The app sign-in is issued within 60 minutes and stays valid for 6 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the mobile app option when something goes wrong.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the app sign-in request.
4. Wait up to 60 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 6 |
| App sign-in valid for | 6 days |
| Typical completion time | 60 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-5213, wait 60 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
6 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-5213.
