# Tax and VAT on invoices (mobile)

## Overview

How tax is calculated and shown in the mobile app. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The tax line is issued within 5 minutes and stays valid for 6 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the tax vat option in the mobile app.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the tax line request.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 7 |
| Tax line valid for | 6 days |
| Typical completion time | 5 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-6362, wait 5 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
7 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-6362.
