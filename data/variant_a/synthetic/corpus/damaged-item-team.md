# Report a damaged item (team)

## Overview

What to do when an item arrives damaged for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The damage report is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 3 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the damaged item option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the damage report request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 12 |
| Damage report valid for | 3 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-4205, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
12 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-4205.
