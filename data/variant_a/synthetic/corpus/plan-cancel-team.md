# Cancel your subscription (team)

## Overview

Cancel at the end of the current billing period for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The cancellation is issued within 30 minutes and stays valid for 2 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the plan cancel option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the cancellation request.
4. Wait up to 30 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 7 |
| Cancellation valid for | 2 days |
| Typical completion time | 30 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-7918, wait 30 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
7 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-7918.
