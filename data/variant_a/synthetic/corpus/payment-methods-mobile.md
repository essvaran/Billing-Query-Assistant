# Manage payment methods (mobile)

## Overview

Add, remove or replace a saved card in the mobile app. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The saved card is issued within 60 minutes and stays valid for 5 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the payment methods option in the mobile app.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the saved card request.
4. Wait up to 60 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 8 |
| Saved card valid for | 5 days |
| Typical completion time | 60 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-3786, wait 60 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
8 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-3786.
