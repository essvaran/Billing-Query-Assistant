# Currency and exchange rates (mobile)

## Overview

Which currency you are charged in in the mobile app. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The exchange rate is issued within 10 minutes and stays valid for 6 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the currency option in the mobile app.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the exchange rate request.
4. Wait up to 10 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 6 |
| Exchange rate valid for | 6 days |
| Typical completion time | 10 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-6689, wait 10 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
6 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-6689.
