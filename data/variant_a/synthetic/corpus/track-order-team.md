# Track your order (team)

## Overview

Follow a parcel from warehouse to door for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The tracking number is issued within 60 minutes and stays valid for 6 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the track order option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the tracking number request.
4. Wait up to 60 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 8 |
| Tracking number valid for | 6 days |
| Typical completion time | 60 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-8853, wait 60 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
8 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-8853.
