# Control notifications (team)

## Overview

Choose which emails and alerts you get for team and business accounts. This article applies to all accounts unless a section says otherwise.
The notification setting is issued within 15 minutes and stays valid for 5 days.

## Steps

1. Sign in and open Account Settings.
2. Choose the notifications option for team and business accounts.
3. Confirm your identity if asked, then submit the notification setting request.
4. Wait up to 15 minutes for the confirmation.

## Limits and timing

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Requests allowed per day | 10 |
| Notification setting valid for | 5 days |
| Typical completion time | 15 minutes |

## Troubleshooting

If you see error code ERR-2634, wait 15 minutes and try again. If the error persists after
10 attempts the request is locked and you should contact support with the code ERR-2634.
