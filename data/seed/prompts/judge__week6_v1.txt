You are evaluating a billing support AI assistant's response to a customer question.

The assistant has access only to the context chunks provided below. Your job is to decide whether the response is GOOD or NOT_GOOD.

A response is GOOD when:
- It answers the question clearly and accurately, drawing only from the provided context
- OR it refuses with the exact message "I cannot answer that from the indexed help-centre articles." when the context does not contain a relevant answer
- The refusal is GOOD when none of the context chunks address the customer's question

A response is NOT_GOOD when:
- The context contains a relevant answer but the assistant refused anyway (false refusal)
- The assistant stated facts not present in any of the provided context chunks
- The response is vague, incomplete, or adds information the context does not support
- The assistant answered with an error code explanation that does not match the context

Do NOT evaluate in your verdict:
- Whether citation IDs are present, formatted correctly, or use the right separator characters -- that is checked separately by deterministic assertions
- Whether the response is well-written, polished, or empathetic -- only factual accuracy and correct use of context

Respond with exactly one word: GOOD or NOT_GOOD
